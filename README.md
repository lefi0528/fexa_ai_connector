# Fexa AI Connector for PrestaShop — AI SEO & Voice Search (AEO)

**Official MCP Server for [Fexa AI](https://fexaai.com)** — the AI platform that optimizes your PrestaShop store for **search engines, voice assistants and AI answer engines.**

Connect your store in one click and let AI automatically improve your SEO, structured data and **voice search readiness (AEO — Answer Engine Optimization)**.

> Keywords: PrestaShop SEO module, AI SEO, voice search optimization, AEO, structured data, JSON-LD, FAQ schema, Product schema, rich snippets, Google Assistant, Alexa, Siri, ChatGPT, Google SGE.

## ✨ Features

### 🎙️ Voice Search & Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)
- **Speakable schema** so assistants (Google Assistant, Alexa, Siri) can read your content aloud.
- **FAQ structured data** that powers spoken answers and Google's "People also ask".
- Content shaped for **AI answer engines** (ChatGPT, Google SGE / AI Overviews).

### 📊 Automatic Structured Data (JSON-LD)
- **Product**, **FAQPage** and **BreadcrumbList** schema injected automatically into your `<head>`.
- Enables **rich snippets** (price, availability, ratings, breadcrumbs) in Google results.
- Schema delivery is **verified** on the live page after each optimization.

### 🤖 AI-Powered SEO Automation
- Auto-rewritten **meta titles, meta descriptions, descriptions and ALT tags**.
- Works across **products, categories and CMS pages**.
- All AI content is **HTML-sanitized** before saving — your theme stays safe.

### 🔌 Plug & Play and Secure
- Simple upload-and-go installation, no complex configuration.
- Uses **MCP (Model Context Protocol)** with API-Key authentication.

## ✅ Compatibility

- **PrestaShop 1.7.8, 8.x and 9.x**
- **PHP 8.1+**

## Installation

1. Download the latest release `.zip` from the [Releases page](https://github.com/lefi0528/fexa_ai_connector/releases/latest).
2. Go to your PrestaShop Admin Panel → **Modules → Module Manager**.
3. Click **"Upload a module"** and select the zip file.
4. Open the module's **Configure** page to get your API Key.
5. Paste the API Key into your [Fexa AI Dashboard](https://fexaai.com) to connect your store.
